Few facts about world we live in


Earth is the only planet known to harbor life, with a 4.5-billion-year history,
a 71% water-covered surface, and a unique,
iron-rich core that generates a protective magnetic field.
It is not a perfect sphere but a slightly squished, rotating ellipsoid, and it acts as the largest terrestrial planet in our solar system



about the earth

  • Equatorial Radius: ~6,378 km (3,963 mi)
  • Polar Radius: ~6,357 km (3,950 mi)
  • Equatorial Circumference: ~40,075 km (24,901 mi)


top 3 fact about earth

  1. Rotation period: 24 hours
  2. Revolution period: 365 days
  3. Surface water: 71% covered
